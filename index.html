To open shortcuts in Incognito windows via Alt + Click, Chrome requires granting one-time Incognito permission.
  1. Click the button below to open Chrome's Extension Details page.
  2. Scroll down to the "Allow in Incognito" setting.
  3. Toggle the switch ON.
  4. Return to Shortcut+ and launch any shortcut with Alt + Click or the 3-dots menu!

Core Features & How to Use
Smart Grid vs. Free Placement: Switch between an auto-centering responsive grid or freeform spatial placement where you can place tiles, the Google Logo, and the Search Bar anywhere on screen with Photoshop-style alignment guides.
Category Groups: Organize your shortcuts into folders (Work, Dev, Media). Click the group launch button to open all shortcuts in new tabs, or hold Alt to launch all in Incognito!
Workspace Profiles: Switch between up to 8 distinct workspace profiles (e.g. Work Mode, Personal, Dev) using the header profile switcher. Each profile preserves its own shortcuts, layout, and wallpaper.
Rich Wallpapers & Video: Choose from curated spatial developer desk setups, cyberpunk/lofi themes, or upload your own custom image, GIF, or looping MP4/WebM video wallpaper.
Shortcut Runner (Offline Arcade Game): Press the 🎮 header button or hit R on your keyboard to play an endless arcade runner. Leap and double-jump over your own saved shortcuts, grab coffee slow-mo and star power-ups, and chase your high score!
